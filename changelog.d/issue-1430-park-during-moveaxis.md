### Fixed

- **OnStep: Park and FindHome during or right after a MoveAxis are no longer dropped without an error** (issue #1430): OnStepX refuses a park or a home while an axis moves or brakes, and the driver sent both commands without reading the answer, so a `Park` right after `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` returned success and the mount never parked. Park and FindHome now stop any running motion first and wait until the mount is still (without blocking other calls), read the mount's answer, retry while it reports the mount still in motion, and report any other refusal (for example "no park position set") as `InvalidOperation`. Tests: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park during a MoveAxis stops the move and parks", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park is retried while the mount reports it still in motion", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park gives up when the mount does not stop in time", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park reports why the mount refused it", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park taken by the mount despite a garbled reply succeeds", "OnStep Telescope Driver - FindHome reports why the mount refused it", "OnStep Telescope Driver - FindHome during a MoveAxis stops the move and homes", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park while parking and FindHome while homing send nothing", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park during a GOTO stops the GOTO and parks", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park waiting for the braking does not block other calls or newer commands", "OnStep Telescope Driver - FindHome is retried while the mount reports it still in motion", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park refused by OnStep 4.x reports the reason" and "OnStep Telescope Driver - Park refused by firmware without :GE# is reported without a reason".
